Wall cladding

Facades, 3D walls and stone-look panels that add architectural depth and character.

A well-dressed wall completely changes how a space feels. Our wall cladding and facade tiles add texture, shadow and depth, from a TV feature wall to the full facade of a residence or commercial building.

Interiors

Living room walls, headboards, spa bathrooms and receptions gain presence with travertine, natural stone and large-format marble-look porcelain or 3D relief panels — easy to clean, with none of the sealing natural stone requires.

Facades and exteriors

Facade cladding must withstand sun, rain and temperature swings. We specify the right system: flexible high-performance adhesives, mechanical anchors when height requires them and properly designed movement joints.
