Extraordinary tile deserves an installation to match. At Acabados Contemporáneos you will find every construction accessory your project needs, from the right adhesive to the trim that delivers the perfect edge.

Adhesives

Choose porcelain tile adhesive by format, substrate and area. For large formats and facades we recommend high-performance polymer-modified cementitious adhesives; for ceramic on interior walls, a quality standard adhesive is enough.

Grouts

  • Unsanded grout for 1–3 mm joints
  • Sanded grout for joints over 3 mm
  • Epoxy grout for pools, kitchens and chemical areas
  • Wide color range to match or contrast

Trims, leveling and tools

Tile trims in aluminum, stainless steel or brass protect edges and solve corners and transitions. Leveling clips and wedges, notched trowels, cutters, diamond blades and suction lifters complete the professional kit.
