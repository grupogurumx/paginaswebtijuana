Terraces, gardens, walkways and pools face intense sun, rain, chlorine and sharp temperature swings. Choosing the right outdoor tile is a safety decision as much as a design one.

Slip resistance comes first

Look for porcelain rated R11 or higher for terraces and pool decks, and R12 for ramps or constantly wet areas. Avoid polished finishes outdoors: they become dangerously slippery when wet.

Low absorption and frost resistance

Porcelain with under 0.5% absorption won't crack from water or frost and resists efflorescence — outperforming ceramic and many natural stones outdoors.

What goes inside the pool?

For the pool shell, glass mosaic is the standard: it absorbs no water, resists chemicals and reflects light beautifully. Porcelain certified for immersion is also available. Always use epoxy adhesive and grout.

Recommended styles

  • Limestone or travertine look: Mediterranean elegance and a cooler surface.
  • Anti-slip wood look: deck warmth with no maintenance.
  • Concrete look: contemporary style for minimalist gardens.
  • 20 mm thickness: for installation over gravel, grass or pedestals.
