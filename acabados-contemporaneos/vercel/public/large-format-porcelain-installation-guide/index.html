How to install large-format porcelain tile without mistakes

Substrate, adhesive, back-buttering, leveling and joints: the steps that guarantee a perfect, long-lasting large-format porcelain installation.

Large-format porcelain tile transforms any space, but it demands a precise installation. A 60×120 cm slab or larger won't forgive uneven substrates or the wrong adhesive. Here is what our team recommends on every project.

1. Prepare a perfectly flat substrate

The base must be clean, dry, solid and flat within 3 mm over 2 meters. Correct any unevenness with self-leveling mortar first. A poor substrate is the number-one cause of hollow tiles, lippage and cracks.

2. Choose the right adhesive

Use a high-performance polymer-modified cementitious porcelain tile adhesive (C2 or higher). Outdoors and on facades, choose a deformable adhesive (S1 or S2) to absorb thermal movement.

3. Back-butter every slab

Spread adhesive on the substrate with a 10–12 mm notched trowel and apply a thin coat on the back of the slab. The goal is over 90% coverage with no voids.

4. Use a leveling system

Leveling clips and wedges prevent lippage while the adhesive cures — essential for large and polished formats where any unevenness shows in the light.

5. Respect the joints

  • Tile joints: at least 1.5–2 mm for rectified porcelain.
  • Perimeter joints: 5–8 mm at walls and columns.
  • Movement joints: every 25–36 m² indoors and every 16 m² outdoors.

6. Grout and clean

Wait for the curing time specified by the manufacturer, use grout suited to the joint width and clean residue before it hardens. In wet or high-traffic areas, consider epoxy grout.

Need the full kit? Adhesive, leveling system, trowels, suction lifters, cutters, grout and tile trims — we calculate everything when we quote your tile.
