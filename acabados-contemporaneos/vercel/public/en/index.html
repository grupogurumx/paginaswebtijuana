FAQ

Your questions, answered

What is the difference between ceramic and porcelain tile?

Porcelain is made from finer clays fired at higher temperatures, so it absorbs less than 0.5% water and resists wear and frost better. Ceramic is ideal for walls and light-traffic areas, with a wide range of glazes at a lower cost.

How much extra material should I buy?

We recommend 5% to 10% extra for cuts and replacements; up to 15% for diagonal, herringbone or large-format layouts. Our team calculates it with you at no cost.

Which tile is best for outdoors and pools?

An anti-slip porcelain paver (R11 or higher) with low absorption, UV and temperature resistance. For pool interiors, glass mosaic or porcelain rated for immersion.

Do you deliver and serve projects outside the city?

Yes. We schedule site deliveries and ship to multiple states across Mexico. Send us your project location to quote shipping.
