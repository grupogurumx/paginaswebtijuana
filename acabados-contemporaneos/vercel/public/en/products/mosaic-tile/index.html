Mosaic tile

Glass, stone and metal for feature walls, pools, showers and details that get noticed.

Mosaic tile is the detail that turns a bathroom into a jewel and a pool into a mirror of water. Small pieces of glass, stone or metal that play with light and add unique personality.

Pool mosaic tile

Glass mosaic is the standard for pool and spa interiors: it absorbs no water, resists chemicals and keeps its color in the sun. Available in blues, turquoises, greens and gradient blends.

Decorative mosaics

In bathrooms, powder rooms, backsplashes and shower niches, stone, metallic or iridescent mosaics create an unforgettable focal point. For submerged areas we recommend epoxy adhesives and grouts, available in Installation accessories.
