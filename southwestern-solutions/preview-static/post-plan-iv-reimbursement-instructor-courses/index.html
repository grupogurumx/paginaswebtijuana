POST Plan IV Reimbursement: How Agencies Fund Instructor Courses

October 20, 2026 · 1 min read

Training budgets are always under pressure. For California agencies, the POST reimbursement plans can make a significant difference — and Plan IV is one of the most commonly used for specialized instructor courses.

What Plan IV Covers

Plan IV reimburses tuition for eligible POST-certified courses. It does not reimburse travel or per diem, so agencies should plan those costs separately.

How to Confirm Eligibility

  • Find the course and presenter in the California POST course catalog.
  • Confirm the specific session is listed as Plan IV reimbursable.
  • Work with your agency training manager to submit the required requests on time.

Ways to Stretch Your Budget

  • Train instructors, not just users. One certified instructor can train many officers in-house.
  • Host a course. Hosting reduces travel for your own officers. See our Host a Course page.
  • Plan ahead. Instructor courses fill quickly — reserving early protects your training calendar.

Southwestern Solutions Courses

Our Chemical Agent Instructor Course and Less Lethal Instructor Course are POST certified and listed as Plan IV reimbursable. Questions? Contact our team.

Reimbursement rules are set by California POST and may change. Always confirm current policies with POST and your agency.
