Tile trends 2026: what you'll see in the most elegant spaces

Earth tones, large formats, natural textures and seamless surfaces: the floor and wall trends shaping interior design this year.

Interior design is moving away from cold and generic. Tile trends for 2026 favor spaces that feel warm, authentic and serene, without sacrificing the technical performance daily life demands. These are the trends we see most in residential and commercial projects.

1. Earth tones and warm palettes

Terracotta, sand, ochre, olive and soft browns are replacing grey as the go-to neutral, creating welcoming spaces that pair beautifully with wood, linen and plants.

2. Large formats and seamless surfaces

Large-format porcelain slabs keep growing: one vein running across floor, wall, island and countertop. Fewer joints mean more visual space and clean architectural lines.

3. Natural textures: stone, travertine and limestone

Matte, textured stone looks dominate spa bathrooms, facades and terraces. Travertine in particular is enjoying a renaissance thanks to its warmth and timeless character.

4. Micro-cement look

Micro-cement-look porcelain delivers the minimalist, continuous feel of polished concrete with better stain resistance and no cracking.

5. Handcrafted ceramics with imperfections

Glazes with tonal variation, irregular edges and zellige effects bring personality to backsplashes and bathrooms.

6. Mosaics take center stage

Mosaics leave the pool to become jewel-like walls: emerald greens, golds and iridescent pieces in powder rooms, bars and niches.

7. Herringbone and chevron wood looks

Wood-look porcelain in herringbone or chevron adds classic sophistication with zero maintenance.

8. Indoor–outdoor continuity

The same design in the living room and on the terrace, switching only to an anti-slip finish, makes spaces feel larger and connected to the garden.

Follow trends without dating your space

Our rule: go timeless on floors and play with trends on walls, backsplashes and details, which are easier to refresh. Want to see these collections in person? Book a showroom visit.
