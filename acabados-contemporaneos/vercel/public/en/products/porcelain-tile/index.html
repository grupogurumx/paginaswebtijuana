Porcelain tile

Large formats, low absorption and lasting beauty: marble, stone and concrete-look porcelain for floors and walls.

Porcelain tile is the most versatile and durable surface for floors and walls. Its dense body, fired at very high temperatures, absorbs less than 0.5% water, making it ideal for high-traffic areas, bathrooms, kitchens and outdoor flooring.

Large-format porcelain slabs

Large-format slabs (from 60×120 cm up to 120×260 cm and beyond) reduce visible joints, make rooms feel larger and allow seamless continuity across floors, walls, countertops and kitchen islands.

Finishes and styles

  • Marble look: Calacatta, Carrara, Statuario, Nero Marquina and onyx, polished or matte.
  • Stone look: travertine, limestone, slate and basalt for interiors and facades.
  • Concrete and micro-cement look: industrial, minimalist aesthetics.
  • Anti-slip: R10, R11 and R12 surfaces for terraces, pools and ramps.
  • Rectified: precise edges for 1–2 mm joints.
  • Polished and satin: mirror shine or silky touch.

How to choose the right porcelain

1. By traffic

For commercial or high-traffic areas, choose full-body or high-abrasion glazed porcelain. For homes, quality glazed porcelain performs beautifully.

2. By area

Outdoors and in wet areas, prioritize anti-slip finishes. In living rooms and bedrooms, choose polished or matte to match your style.

3. By installation

Large formats require high-performance cementitious adhesive, a leveling system and a perfectly flat substrate. We advise you on the complete installation system.
