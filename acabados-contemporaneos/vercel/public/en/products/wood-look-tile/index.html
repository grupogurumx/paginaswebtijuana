Wood-look tile

The warmth of natural wood with the strength, durability and low maintenance of porcelain.

Wood-look porcelain tile faithfully reproduces the grain, knots and tones of oak, walnut or ash — without the drawbacks of real wood: it resists scratches, doesn't swell with moisture and never needs sanding or varnish.

Advantages over real wood

  • Waterproof: perfect for bathrooms, kitchens and outdoors
  • No termites, no periodic maintenance
  • Compatible with radiant floor heating
  • Highly resistant to traffic and pets
  • Indoor–outdoor continuity with anti-slip versions
  • Planks from 20×120 cm up to 30×180 cm

Installation patterns

Staggered planks are a timeless classic; herringbone and chevron bring European sophistication to living rooms, lobbies and retail. We calculate the waste for each pattern, which can reach 15%.
