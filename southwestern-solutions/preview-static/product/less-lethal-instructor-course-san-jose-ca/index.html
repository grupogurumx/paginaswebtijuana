Description

Course Overview

The Less Lethal Instructor Course is a 30-hour, POST-certified instructor course that builds officers into instructors capable of running safe, defensible kinetic energy projectile programs. Taught by former and current Law Enforcement Tactical Operators with over 50 years of experience.

Southwestern Solutions Chemical Agent and Less Lethal Instructor Courses meet AB 48 requirements and are POST certified. This course also covers AB 48 requirements and compliance.

What You Will Learn

  • Legal standards, use-of-force policy and AB 48 / Penal Code 13652
  • Less-lethal platforms, munitions and their characteristics
  • Target areas, distances and prohibited targeting (head, neck, vital organs)
  • Marksmanship, malfunctions and safe range operations
  • Medical considerations and post-deployment procedures
  • Documentation, reporting and instructor methodology

Frequently Asked Questions

Is this course POST Plan IV reimbursable?

Yes. The course is POST certified and Plan IV reimbursable (tuition). Travel and per diem are not reimbursed under Plan IV.

Does the course cover AB 48?

Yes. AB 48 / Penal Code 13652 requirements are covered so your instructors can carry compliant lesson plans and documentation back to the agency.

Can I register several officers?

Yes. Increase the number of seats before adding the course to your cart, then list every attendee at checkout.
