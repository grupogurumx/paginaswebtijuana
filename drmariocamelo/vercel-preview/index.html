FAQ

Answers before you decide

Am I a candidate for bariatric surgery?

Generally, adults with a BMI of 35 or more, or 30–35 with obesity-related conditions such as type 2 diabetes or hypertension, may be candidates. Final eligibility is decided after a medical evaluation.

How much weight can I expect to lose?

On average, gastric sleeve patients lose about 25–30% of total body weight and gastric bypass patients about 30–32%. Results vary with each patient and their commitment to follow-up.

Is it safe to have surgery in Tijuana?

Safety depends on the surgeon, the team and the hospital. Dr. Camelo is a certified bariatric surgeon with more than 10,000 surgeries, operates with a multidisciplinary team and provides structured follow-up after you return home.

Can the gallbladder be removed during bariatric surgery?

In some patients it can be evaluated, but every case must be individualized. Rapid weight loss can favor gallstone formation, so we assess it before surgery.

Yes. You can apply online in a few minutes through our secure financing form and our team will contact you with the available options.
