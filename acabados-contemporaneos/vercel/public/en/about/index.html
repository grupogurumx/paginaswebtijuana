About us

For over 35 years, dressing Mexico's most demanding spaces with surfaces built to last.

Acabados Contemporáneos is a Mexican tile supplier with more than 35 years of experience in ceramic tile, porcelain tile, floor and wall coverings and construction accessories. We were founded on a simple belief: the right finish turns a built space into a place people enjoy for decades.

A track record built piece by piece

For more than three decades we have worked alongside homeowners renovating their homes, architects searching for the exact material for their vision, and builders who need stock, pricing and on-time delivery. That experience delivers something no catalog can: knowing what works in each area, which format fits, how porcelain performs outdoors and which accessories guarantee a flawless installation.

What defines us

  • Honest, expert advice. We recommend what your project needs, not the most expensive option.
  • Curated collections. Large-format porcelain, handcrafted ceramics, mosaics, stone and wood looks.
  • Stock and batch consistency. Essential for large and phased projects.
  • One-stop solution. Floor, wall, adhesive, grout, trims and tools in one place.
  • Project pricing. Tailored quotes for construction and design professionals.
  • After-sales support. We stay with you after delivery.

Mission

To provide quality tiles and construction accessories, backed by specialized advice and personal service, so every client achieves beautiful, functional and lasting spaces.

Vision

To be Mexico's reference for anyone seeking design, quality and trust in floors and walls, combining more than 35 years of experience with the latest trends in interior design and architecture.

A great finish isn't noticed — it's felt. It is the difference between building a space and creating a place.
