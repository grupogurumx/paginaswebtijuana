## 2026-06-26 - A11y and Focus States for Icon-Only Buttons
**Learning:** Implementing accessible focus rings across dark and light backgrounds requires careful `ring-offset` adjustments using Tailwind's `focus-visible` utility to ensure visibility and contrast.
**Action:** Use `focus-visible:ring-offset-gray-900` for dark backgrounds (like the footer or hero section) and standard rings for light backgrounds to ensure clear focus indicators for keyboard navigation.
