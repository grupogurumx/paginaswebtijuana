Ceramic tile is the perfect partner for bathroom walls, kitchens and spaces where color and texture take center stage. Lightweight, easy to install and available in endless designs, it offers outstanding value.

Bathroom wall tile

From timeless whites to greens, blues and terracottas with a handcrafted effect, our bathroom tiles resist moisture and clean easily. Pair them with porcelain floors for a cohesive, durable space.

Kitchen backsplashes

A ceramic backsplash protects walls from splashes and gives the kitchen character. Subway, herringbone, zellige or hexagon formats create textures that catch the light.

Ceramic or porcelain?

For interior walls, ceramic is ideal. For medium to high-traffic floors, outdoors or very wet areas, we recommend porcelain tile. Read our full guide: Ceramic vs. porcelain tile.
