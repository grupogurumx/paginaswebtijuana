In hospitality and retail, floors and walls are part of the brand. A striking lobby makes the first impression; a floor that wears out in two years ruins it. Specifying tile for hotels and commercial spaces means balancing design, durability and maintenance.

Heavy traffic without wear

Choose full-body or high-abrasion glazed porcelain for lobbies, corridors and public areas. In restaurants and kitchens, prioritize anti-slip surfaces and epoxy grouts that resist grease and intensive cleaning.

The impact of large formats

A large-format onyx or marble-look wall, even backlit, becomes the visual signature of the space — at a fraction of the weight, cost and upkeep of natural stone.

Specification checklist

  • Abrasion resistance matched to traffic
  • Slip resistance at entrances, restrooms, kitchens and pools
  • Formats that reduce joints and cleaning time
  • Complete system: adhesive, grout, trims and movement joints
  • Spare stock for future maintenance (3% to 5%)
